Privacy Policy

This policy explains how KoboAds collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our advertising and rewards platform.

Effective date: September 2, 2026

1. Who we are

KoboAds is a mobile advertising marketplace that helps businesses create campaigns and allows earners to discover advertisements and receive rewards. In this policy, “KoboAds,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the KoboAds service.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account and profile details, such as your name, phone number, email address, account type, and profile information.
  • Campaign information, including ad copy, targeting choices, budgets, business details, and campaign performance preferences.
  • Content you upload, such as images, videos, thumbnails, and other advertising media.
  • Wallet and payout information, including transaction history and details needed to process withdrawals or deposits. Payment credentials are handled by our payment providers and are not stored by KoboAds unless expressly stated.
  • Messages and support requests you send to us.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information, such as device type, operating system, app version, language, and basic diagnostics.
  • Usage and delivery information, such as screens viewed, ad interactions, campaign impressions, reward activity, and timestamps.
  • Notification information, such as a device push-notification token when you grant notification permission.
  • Technical information sent by your browser or device when you use the web version, including approximate network information and log data.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide, maintain, and improve KoboAds; create and deliver advertising campaigns; measure campaign performance; calculate and issue rewards; process payments and withdrawals; authenticate accounts; send service and notification messages; prevent fraud, abuse, and policy violations; moderate uploaded content; respond to support requests; and comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate KoboAds, such as Firebase for authentication, database, functions, and file storage; payment providers such as Paystack when needed to process a payment or payout; notification and infrastructure providers; analytics and security providers; and professional advisers or authorities when required by law.

We do not sell your personal information. Advertisers may receive campaign performance information and aggregated or campaign-related delivery data, but we do not provide them with your private account credentials or payment credentials.

5. Advertising content and moderation

Uploaded advertising media may be stored and reviewed to operate campaigns, protect the community, and enforce our advertising and distribution rules. We may remove or restrict content that is unlawful, misleading, unsafe, or otherwise violates those rules.

6. Payments and rewards

KoboAds may use third-party payment processors to facilitate deposits, withdrawals, and other payment-related services. Those providers may collect and process information under their own privacy policies. Payment processing, eligibility, fees, and payout timing may also be subject to applicable provider terms.

7. Notifications and choices

You can control push notifications through your device settings. You can also contact us about account information, marketing communications, or other privacy choices. Some service messages are necessary to operate your account and may still be sent when you disable promotional notifications.

8. Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including account administration, financial records, dispute handling, security, and legal compliance. We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or obtain a copy of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request, and some information may need to be retained for legal or security reasons.

10. Children’s privacy

KoboAds is not intended for children under 13. If local law requires a higher minimum age to use a service involving rewards or payments, that higher age applies. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children who are not permitted to use the service.

11. Third-party links and services

KoboAds may link to or rely on third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and this policy does not apply to information those services collect independently. Please review their policies before using them.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as KoboAds changes or as legal requirements develop. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the effective date. Your continued use of KoboAds after an update means the revised policy applies to your use of the service.

13. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact KoboAds at support@koboads.com.
